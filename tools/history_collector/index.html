Shorts Watch History Exporter

Export your recent YouTube Shorts watch history to support our research on personalized multi-agent video storyboarding. Scans up to 90 recent Shorts (3 subsets of 30).

Client-side only: This tool runs directly in your browser. It does not access your Google credentials, passwords, or personal account data. Only publicly available video titles and URLs from your history tab are exported.
How to Export Your Watch History
1
Drag this button into your Bookmarks Bar:

Click, hold, and drag the red button upward into your browser's bookmarks bar (do not just click it here).

Export Shorts History
Bookmarks bar hidden? Press Ctrl + Shift + B (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + B (Mac).
2
Open YouTube History:

Navigate to youtube.com/feed/history while logged in.

3
Click the bookmark & download:

Click Export Shorts History in your bookmarks bar while on YouTube. A floating panel will collect up to 90 recent Shorts (or click Export Now anytime), then click Download JSON to save youtube_shorts_history.json.

4
Send the JSON file back:

Send your downloaded youtube_shorts_history.json file to the researcher who invited you.
