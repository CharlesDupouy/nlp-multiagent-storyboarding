Shorts Watch History Exporter

Export your recent YouTube Shorts watch history to support our research on personalized multi-agent video storyboarding. Scans deeply to collect exactly 90 recent Shorts (3 subsets of 30), or click "Export Now" anytime.

Client-side only: This tool runs directly in your browser. It does not access your Google credentials, passwords, or personal account data. Only publicly available video titles and URLs from your history tab are exported.
