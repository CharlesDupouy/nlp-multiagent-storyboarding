YouTube Shorts History Exporter

Export your recent Shorts watch history in 10 seconds to help us build personalized AI video agents.

🔒
100% Private & Client-Side: This script runs purely inside your browser tab. It never accesses your Google account, passwords, or personal credentials. Only public video titles and links are saved into your download.
🚀 3-Step Exporter (Drag & Click)
1
Drag this button to your Bookmarks Bar:

Click and hold the red button below, then drag it up into your browser's bookmarks bar.

📌 Export Shorts History
Bookmarks bar hidden? Press Ctrl + Shift + B (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + B (Mac) to show it.
2
Open YouTube History:

Open youtube.com/feed/history in a new tab (make sure you are logged in).

3
Click the bookmark on YouTube:

While on your YouTube History tab, click 📌 Export Shorts History in your bookmarks bar. A panel will appear, auto-scroll to find your shorts, and give you a Download button!
